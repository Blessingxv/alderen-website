ENGINEERING INTELLIGENCE

Engineering workflows, rethought.

Engineering teams rely on complex simulation workflows to make critical design decisions. Alderen is exploring how intelligent software can help make these workflows more efficient, accessible, and connected.

Explore our approach ↓

An early-stage initiative focused on engineering workflow challenges.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Better engineering decisions shouldn't mean unnecessary delays.

Simulation-driven engineering can involve demanding computational work, repeated iterations, and time-consuming analysis.

When a simulation produces an unexpected result, engineers may need to revisit assumptions, adjust designs, and run further analyses before moving forward.

The opportunity is to understand where these workflows create friction — and where intelligent tools could help.

02 / OUR APPROACH

A more intelligent way to approach engineering work.

Alderen is investigating an agentic engineering software platform designed to coordinate useful capabilities across simulation-driven workflows.

01

Intelligent orchestration

Explore how software agents could help coordinate stages of an engineering workflow, from preparing tasks to interpreting results.

02

Simulation-aware tools

Investigate how engineering-specific tools could support analysis, reduce repetitive work, and help engineers navigate complex tasks.

03

Surrogate modelling

Explore validated surrogate models as one potential capability for approximating simulation results where appropriate.

These capabilities are areas of development and exploration, not claims of an already deployed product.
